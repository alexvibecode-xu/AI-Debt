Market says whether the instrument trades: Traded bond = senior or convertible notes sold to investors (Bloomberg has a price); Syndicated loan = broadly syndicated term loan that trades in the loan market; Private = bank facilities, private credit, leases and vendor financing with no market price. Traded rows show their Bloomberg ISIN (one per tranche); BBG ID: NA means Bloomberg has no record of the instrument yet. The per-tranche list is in data/bonds.csv.

Rating comes from one source, ccir.io's rated GPU-collateral financings, matched to deals by borrower, date and size. Hover a rating for the facility name, the date it entered the public record and the offtaker. A dash means no public agency grade is on record there, not that the deal is unrated.

Data scraped from American Compute's GPU Financing Tracker. Amounts are as announced; the source site's own headline total counts new money only, so it can be lower than the sum shown here. Convertible notes, senior notes, loans and leases are all included; filter by type to narrow.

Deal data refreshes automatically every weekday morning (GitHub Actions re-runs the scraper and republishes this page); market data is pushed after each Bloomberg close.

Market data comes from the History sheet of the AI Debt Tracker workbook, which pulls daily price and yield per bond from Bloomberg. To refresh: open the workbook on the Bloomberg machine, wait for the History sheet to fill, save, close, then run Export market data.cmd and reload this page. Change windows are computed from that history: 1D vs. the prior close, WTD vs. last Friday, MTD vs. prior month-end, YTD vs. December 31, 3M/6M/9M/12M vs. the same calendar day, since issue vs. the first available price.

Bonds that share an ISIN (add-on taps) appear once. Bonds without a Bloomberg ID are listed on the Tracker tab as "BBG ID: NA" and are not here. The Rating column here is the bond-level agency rating from Bloomberg (Moody's / S&P / Fitch); "unrated" means none of the three rates that bond, which is common for convertibles. The Tracker tab's Rating column is the ccir.io facility grade instead.
