Market says whether the instrument trades: Traded bond = senior or convertible notes sold to investors (Bloomberg has a price); Syndicated loan = broadly syndicated term loan that trades in the loan market; Private = bank facilities, private credit, leases and vendor financing with no market price. Traded rows show their Bloomberg ISIN (one per tranche); BBG ID: NA means Bloomberg has no record of the instrument yet. The per-tranche list is in data/bonds.csv.

Rating comes from one source, ccir.io's rated GPU-collateral financings, matched to deals by borrower, date and size. Hover a rating for the facility name, the date it entered the public record and the offtaker. A dash means no public agency grade is on record there, not that the deal is unrated.

Data scraped from American Compute's GPU Financing Tracker. Amounts are as announced; the source site's own headline total counts new money only, so it can be lower than the sum shown here. Convertible notes, senior notes, loans and leases are all included; filter by type to narrow.

Data refreshes automatically every weekday morning (GitHub Actions re-runs the scraper and republishes this page).
